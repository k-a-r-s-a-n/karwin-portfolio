Bundled web fonts — self-hosted so the app builds and runs without network access
to fonts.googleapis.com (also removes a render-blocking third-party round trip).

- space-grotesk-latin.woff2  — Space Grotesk (variable, latin subset)
- jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2 — JetBrains Mono (variable, latin subset)

Both licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Upstream sources: https://github.com/fontsource/fontsource
